Fast Printing Instructions

1. Seamless POS Printing:

When you click "Save & Print Bill" on the billing screen, the app immediately saves the bill and invokes the print action in an invisible background frame.

2. Zero-Dialog / 1-Click Printing in Chrome / Edge (Kiosk Mode):

To make Google Chrome or Edge print straight to your thermal printer with zero dialogs (instant 0-second print):

--kiosk-printing

Add --kiosk-printing to your Chrome shortcut target or launch Chrome with kiosk printing enabled. The browser will automatically send receipts directly to your default thermal printer without asking.

3. Save Only Option:

Click "Save Only" whenever a customer does not need a paper receipt. The bill will be saved to database and the POS screen resets instantly.
