Counter PC Print Service Setup Guide

How the PC-Based Wi-Fi Thermal Print Integration Works:

  1. The USB thermal printer is connected to your Counter PC.
  2. Run pc_print_service.py in the background on the Counter PC.
  3. Staff on their Android phone connect to the shop Wi-Fi and tap "Save & Print" on the mobile app.
  4. The backend queues the thermal receipt. The PC service instantly picks it up and prints it out with zero clicks or dialogs!
